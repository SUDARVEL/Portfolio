# How to fill your portfolio (plain English)

Your full worksheet is in [`STORYTELLING_STRUCTURE.md`](./STORYTELLING_STRUCTURE.md).

## The best storytelling idea (remember this)

**Show the ending first, then prove how you got there.**

Like a movie trailer:
1. What you shipped (pretty final screens)
2. What changed (impact)
3. What was broken (challenge)
4. What you decided (short process)
5. What you learned

Do this for **only 3 projects** that are:
- **Relevant** to the job you want
- **Remarkable** (clear thinking + craft)
- **Recent** (shows who you are now)
